⚠️ Anti-Scam Public Advisory

How citizens get scammed every day across India:

  • Fake Portals: Scam sites charging ₹500 for Aadhaar PVC or PAN cards. Official PVC card fee is only ₹50.
  • Cyber-Café Overcharging: Agents charging ₹200 for basic e-Aadhaar or Voter ID downloads which are 100% free on government portals.
  • OTP Theft: Middlemen asking for your Aadhaar OTP. Never share your Aadhaar OTP with any agent or over the phone.
  • Tatkaal Passport Scams: Agents claiming guaranteed appointment booking for ₹3,000 extra. All appointments open daily on official Passport Seva portal at designated times.
